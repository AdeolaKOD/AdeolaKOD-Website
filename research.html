Research

My research interests are in human-computer interaction, particularly how older adults perceive on-screen feedback and remember gestural interfaces.

Interests

  • Perception of on-screen feedback in ageing users
  • Memorability of gestural interfaces, and user-defined versus designer-defined gestures
  • Touchless and assistive input with ordinary cameras
  • HCI research with older adults in Nigeria

Theses and projects

  1. Cognitive Vision-Based Hand Gesture Recognition System for Media and Accessibility Control

    Adeola Kola-Olaleye. Undergraduate thesis, Nile University of Nigeria, 2026. Supervised by Mrs Aisha Lawal.

    A webcam-only system for controlling volume, brightness and media playback with hand gestures, running at a mean latency of 35 ms on a laptop CPU. Selected for the faculty thesis exhibition.

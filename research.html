How older adults perceive on-screen feedback and remember gestural interfaces, and whether gestures they define themselves are easier to recall.

Thesis: controlling a computer with just a webcam

Nile University of Nigeria, 2025–2026 Selected for the faculty thesis exhibition Thesis report (PDF) Code on GitHub

A webcam-only system that lets people control volume, brightness and media playback with hand gestures, built with Python, OpenCV and Google MediaPipe.

I chose every gesture myself. That left me wondering whether older adults could see the feedback clearly or remember the gestures, which is what I want to study next.

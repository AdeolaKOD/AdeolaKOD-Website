Cognitive Vision-Based Hand Gesture Recognition System

2025

Undergraduate Thesis / Final Year Project, Abuja, Nigeria

  • Developed a two-handed gesture system that controls system volume, brightness, and media playback on a consumer CPU. This was achieved by incorporating geometry mappings and focusing only on the necessary physical actions of hand landmarks mapped to digital actions to reduce the user’s cognitive load. Average latency of 35 ms; ∼28–30 FPS in good lighting, ∼15–20 FPS in low light on consumer-grade CPUs.
  • Implemented geometric regression algorithms, linear interpolation, and palm-depth proxies (Z-axis) alongside a gesture clutch mechanism to eliminate false activations and tremor-induced signal jitter.
  • Selected to showcase the system at the faculty thesis exhibition panel.

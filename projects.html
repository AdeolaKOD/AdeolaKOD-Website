Touchless gesture control

Python, OpenCV, Google MediaPipe

My undergraduate thesis. MediaPipe tracks both hands from a standard webcam: the right hand sets volume from the thumb–index distance and brightness from apparent palm size, the left hand controls playback, and a folded-finger clutch blocks tremor and stray movement. It runs at 28–30 FPS with a mean end-to-end latency of 35 ms on a consumer CPU, with no GPU or depth sensor.

Robot teleoperation with ROS

ROS Noetic, Ubuntu, Raspberry Pi 3, Arduino

Controller-driven teleoperation for a CamJam EduKit chassis robot on a Raspberry Pi 3 running Ubuntu and ROS Noetic. I configured the teleoperation nodes and the publisher/subscriber pipeline that carries controller input to the motors, integrated an Arduino microcontroller, and verified signals with a digital multimeter and a logic analyser.

Pathfinding visualiser

Python, Pygame

An interactive grid where you draw walls or generate a random maze, then run A* (with a Manhattan-distance heuristic), Dijkstra's algorithm, breadth-first or depth-first search. The open set, visited cells and final path animate in real time, with live counts of nodes visited and path length.

Flutter, Dart

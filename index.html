Making touchless interfaces work for older adults

See my research Download CV

I study how older adults perceive and remember touchless, gesture-based interfaces. My undergraduate thesis at Nile University of Nigeria built a webcam-only system for real-time, two-handed computer control, and my proposed MSc research compares the memorability of user-defined and designer-defined gestures for adults over 65 under different forms of visual feedback.

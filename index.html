Making touchless interfaces work for older adults

See my research View projects

I am a final-year undergraduate in Computer Science at Nile University of Nigeria. My research interests are in human-computer interaction, specifically how older adults perceive on-screen feedback and remember gestural interfaces, and whether gestures they define themselves are easier to recall.

For my undergraduate thesis, supervised by Mrs Aisha Lawal, I designed and built a webcam-only hand-gesture system that controls a computer in real time. The project was selected for the faculty thesis exhibition.

Before transferring to Nile, I studied Computer Science at the University of Pécs in Hungary, where I twice received the Dean's Merit Award. I have also worked as an automation developer intern at ExxonMobil in Budapest and as a software developer intern at WebSoft in Lagos.
